Collaboration

AIDrift is developed in collaboration with the Pocket Racer team and builds on their open-source, head-to-head autonomous racing platform for AI education.

🏎️

Pocket Racer

An open-source, pocket-sized racing platform for head-to-head autonomous racing in classrooms and hallways — the project AIDrift shares its roots, tools, and team spirit with.

pocketracers.github.io →

💨

AIDrift

Where Pocket Racer studies high-speed racing and overtaking, AIDrift pushes the same miniature platforms past the limit of grip — into controlled, autonomous drift.
