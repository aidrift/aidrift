3D-Printed Parts

All STL files are in the repository. PLA works fine; print drift rings in a harder plastic (PETG/ABS) for consistent grip.

📦

Where to Buy

Everything is available from common electronics distributors and hobby shops. No custom PCBs or machined parts required.

🏁

The Track

Any smooth, hard floor works — laminate, linoleum, sealed concrete. Mark the course with tape; a 2×3 m area is enough for full drift maneuvers.
